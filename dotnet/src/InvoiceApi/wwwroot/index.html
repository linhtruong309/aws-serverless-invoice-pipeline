Invoice checker

Every invoice gets its math checked before you trust it.

Drop a PDF invoice into the pipeline and it reads every line, adds it all up again and checks the VAT. Invoices that add up are trusted. Anything that doesn't goes to a person. Here is what it found in the sample invoices. They are made up for this demo.
